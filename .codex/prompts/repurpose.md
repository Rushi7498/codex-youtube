# Cross-Platform Repurposing

Transform the supplied video/script into platform-tailored assets (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, blog/email as requested).

For each asset, identify the source moment or paragraph, rewrite rather than blindly cross-post, tailor length and CTA, and preserve factual meaning. Do not fabricate exact clip timestamps unless source timing/transcript supports them. Confirm user has reuse rights before using third-party footage.
